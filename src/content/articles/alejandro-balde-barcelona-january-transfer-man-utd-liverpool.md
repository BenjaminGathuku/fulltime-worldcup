Alejandro Balde's Barcelona career looks increasingly like it is heading for a January exit. The left-back, who was one of the most exciting young defenders in Europe as recently as two years ago, has found himself frozen out under Hansi Flick and is now being linked with a loan move that would take him away from the Camp Nou in the winter window.

## From Starter to Third Choice

The decline in Balde's standing at Barcelona has been rapid. Under previous managers he was a first-choice left-back, a player whose pace and attacking instincts made him a key part of how Barcelona built from the back. Under Flick, those qualities have not been enough to keep him in the team.

He has made just one first-team appearance this season. The coaching staff have reportedly been pursuing alternatives, and the club are understood to be considering bringing in another left-back in 2027 to address the position permanently. For a player who was a Spain international and a Champions League regular, the fall in status has been sudden and significant.

The clearest indicator of where things stood came in the summer, when Balde was left out of Spain's World Cup squad. For a left-back of his profile and age, that was a signal that the national team selectors shared the same doubts Flick had expressed through selection.

## Barcelona Open the Door

The situation has reached a point where Barcelona are willing to let Balde leave on loan in January rather than keep a frustrated player who is not getting game time. His contract runs through 2028 and there have been no talks about an extension, which makes the loan option a way of giving him opportunities while Barcelona retain his registration for a possible summer assessment.

TeamTalk reported that Barcelona have now given Balde permission to explore a January exit, with a loan structure the most likely framework for any deal. The precise terms of potential arrangements remain unclear, but the club's willingness to allow him to leave is confirmed.

## The Three-Club Race

Manchester United have been tracking Balde since the summer, when their interest was not pursued after they prioritised other targets. With the January window approaching and Balde's availability now clearer, United's recruitment team are reconsidering the opportunity. A loan would allow them to assess whether he can rediscover his form without the financial commitment of a permanent deal.

Liverpool have also been offered the chance to sign him, according to reports, and are understood to be considering whether the move fits their left-back plans. AC Milan entered the picture separately, with reports suggesting Chelsea were also monitoring the situation before Milan emerged as a more concrete interest.

The range of clubs involved reflects both Balde's underlying quality and the low-risk nature of a loan proposition. Each club gets to assess a player who, at his best, was one of the best young left-backs in world football.

## What Balde Gives a Club

The case for Balde at any of the Premier League clubs is built on what he showed before this season's difficulties. He is 22 years old. He can play very high up the pitch. His recovery speed gives managers the option to push him forward in possession and trust him to get back into position when the team defends.

Those qualities, combined with the possibility of a loan rather than a permanent commitment, make him an attractive option for clubs looking to add depth or competition at left-back without spending heavily in January.

Whether the Barcelona version of Balde, the one frozen out by Flick and without a competitive run of games since early in the season, can hit those levels immediately at a new club is the question any interested party will need to answer for themselves. January loans are often a gamble. This one has better odds than most, given what the player has previously shown he can do.

## What Happens Next

Balde's January destination will likely be settled by which club makes the most compelling case for regular football. A player in his situation does not need money or a club with a bigger name. He needs games. The club that offers him the clearest route to a starting role will be the one he chooses.

That may ultimately decide between United, Liverpool and Milan more than any other factor. All three have left-back situations that could accommodate him. All three are preparing their offers.
