Harry Kane is on the verge of signing a contract extension with Bayern Munich that would keep him at the club until 2029. Sky Sport Germany insider Florian Plettenberg reports that both sides are ironing out the final details, with the aim of completing the deal before the end of the year.

The timing is not a coincidence. Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or on 27 October, confirming what had been building for months: that his 73-goal, World Cup final season was the best individual campaign by any player in world football. Bayern, having watched him become the best footballer on the planet while wearing their shirt, moved decisively to secure his future.

## What the Extension Means

Kane joined Bayern Munich from Tottenham Hotspur in 2023, signing a contract that ran through 2027. The proposed extension to 2029 would take him to 35 years old at its expiry, meaning Bayern are committing to him through what will be the final stretch of his playing career.

For a club that has historically struggled to retain ageing forwards, the decision reflects both the exceptional nature of Kane's contribution and the difficulty of replacing a player who scored 73 goals in a single season. No natural successor exists on the market at anything approaching his level of output.

The extension also closes the door, at least for now, on speculation that linked him with a move to Saudi Arabia or a return to English football. Bayern have made their position clear: they want Kane for the foreseeable future.

## From Ballon d'Or Night to Contract Renewal

Kane's 2025-26 season was the foundation of everything. Thirty-six Bundesliga goals, the European Golden Shoe as the top scorer across all of Europe's top-flight leagues, six World Cup goals on the way to England's first final since 1966. The Ballon d'Or confirmed the season's standing in historical terms.

Bayern's response to that season is commercial and competitive. Commercially, the Ballon d'Or winner wearing your kit is a marketing and sponsorship asset unlike almost anything else in world football. Competitively, a striker of Kane's quality in a team that is performing this well in the Champions League represents a genuine title threat.

Bayern lead the Champions League table after Matchday 4 with a perfect record. If they are going to win it, they will need Kane scoring goals in the knockout rounds. The contract extension says clearly that Bayern believe that is possible.

## The Remaining Details

Plettenberg's report indicates both parties want the deal done before the end of the calendar year. The specific salary terms and any performance-related clauses have not been reported. What is confirmed is the direction: Kane stays, through 2029, with the full support of the club that handed him the platform to win the game's biggest individual award.

For a player who spent nine years at Tottenham without a trophy and who moved to Germany at 29 to change the narrative of his career, signing a contract extension as the reigning world player of the year at Bayern Munich is a reasonable definition of vindication.
