Matchday 4 of the 2026-27 Champions League will be remembered for a very long time. Across two nights of football, we got late drama in Athens, Liverpool's unbeaten run ended in Istanbul, Manchester City edged through in Leipzig — and in Lens, an Italian club most neutrals would have laughed at as a Champions League participant just 18 months ago rewrote the history books.

Here's everything that happened.

---

## Tuesday, 3 November

**Galatasaray 2–0 Stuttgart**
A composed home win for Galatasaray, who continue to prove that Turkish clubs can compete at this level.

**Atlético Madrid 0–2 Bayern Munich**
Bayern were clinical in Madrid, shutting down Atleti's attack and scoring twice to hand Simeone's side a chastening defeat at the Metropolitano.

**Barcelona 3–0 Aston Villa**
A reminder of Barcelona's class. Villa, who have struggled in this competition, were outclassed by a Barça side that looked every inch the European heavyweights they are. A tough night for Unai Emery's men to reflect on.

**Villarreal 0–6 Paris Saint-Germain**
PSG didn't just win — they demolished. Six unanswered goals against a Villarreal side that simply couldn't cope. PSG now lead the Champions League table with 12 points from four games and a goal difference of +15. They are looking terrifying.

**Manchester United 3–2 Roma**
One of the more entertaining Tuesday night matches. United twice fell behind before fighting back to claim all three points, showing some of the grit that has been absent from their Premier League form. A confidence-boosting result for Ruben Amorim's men.

**Feyenoord 0–3 Inter**
Inter were dominant, clinical and largely untroubled. A statement win that moves them firmly into the top half of the CL table.

**Bodø/Glimt 0–0 Lille**
The least eventful game of the round. Both sides settle for a point in Norway.

**Shakhtar Donetsk 0–1 Sporting CP**
A tight, low-scoring affair won by a single goal for Sporting.

**LASK 4–0 Slovan Bratislava**
An emphatic win for the Austrian side.

---

## Wednesday, 4 November

**Real Madrid 3–2 AEK Athens**
Real Madrid don't do easy, do they? They were twice pegged back by AEK Athens — Koita giving the Greek side the lead (12'), Bellingham levelling (32'), Pilios restoring their advantage (75'), Mbappé equalising again (78') — before Valverde struck deep into stoppage time (90+2') to seal yet another dramatic Madrid win. That's four wins from four, 12 points, and yet another entry in the Valverde late-goal highlight reel.

**Fenerbahçe 3–2 Liverpool**
A result that sent shockwaves through Anfield. Liverpool's young striker Nyoni put them ahead (25'), but Greenwood equalised (29'). Isak restored the lead (69'), Archie Brown levelled (73') — and then Muriqi headed a winner nine minutes later that ended Liverpool's unbeaten Champions League campaign. Liverpool drop to 8th in the CL table on nine points. For Fenerbahçe, it's a seismic scalp.

**RB Leipzig 2–3 Manchester City**
A tense one in Germany. Leipzig made City work for it, but Haaland — naturally — scored the winner in the 78th minute. City are building momentum.

**Slavia Prague 1–2 Arsenal**
Arsenal kept their perfect record intact, winning in Prague to move to 10 points from four games. The defending Premier League champions are now also the form team in the Champions League. No scorers reported officially, but the result speaks for itself.

**Porto 4–1 Napoli**
A big Porto win, with Bednarek, Veiga and Kiwior among the scorers. McTominay got a consolation for Napoli. A brutal night for the Italian club.

**Borussia Dortmund 2–1 Real Betis**
Karetsas was the Dortmund hero, scoring the winner in the 72nd minute after Betis had levelled through Ruibal.

**PSV Eindhoven 1–2 Club Brugge**
An away win for Club Brugge, who continue to punch above their weight in this competition.

**Viking Stavanger 4–1 Sabah**
A thumping win for the Norwegian side.

## The Talking Points

**PSG vs Real Madrid: Who wins the title?**
Both sides are on 12 points with a 100% record, but PSG's goal difference of +15 against Real Madrid's +6 is stunning. PSG are not just winning — they're humiliating opponents. If those two meet in the knockout rounds, it will be appointment television.

**Liverpool's reality check**
Iraola's side have shown real quality in the Premier League this season, but Fenerbahçe exposed some defensive fragility. The manner of the defeat — letting in a 79th-minute header after going ahead — will concern the Anfield dressing room.

**Arsenal's quiet dominance**
Four wins, 10 points, unbeaten. Arsenal are doing this the quiet, efficient way. No flashy 9-1s, just methodical wins. That might actually be more concerning for their rivals.
