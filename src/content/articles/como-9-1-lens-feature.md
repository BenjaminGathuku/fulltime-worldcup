There are results, and then there are *results*. Lens 1–9 Como on Wednesday night falls firmly, spectacularly, and almost incomprehensibly into the second category.

This wasn't a Champions League win. This was a statement. A reckoning. The most emphatic away victory in the 32-year history of the Champions League era, delivered by a club from a lakeside Italian town that was playing in Serie B as recently as the 2022-23 season.

Let that sink in for a moment.

---

## How Did It Happen?

Lens arrived at the Stade Bollaert-Delélis having lost their three previous Champions League group games. They were already effectively out. The pressure was off — or at least, it should have been a comfortable, low-stakes evening for a mid-table Ligue 1 side hosting an Italian newcomer.

It was not.

**Nicolas Douvikas** — the Greek striker who has quietly been one of the most underrated forwards in European football this season — had the game of his life. His hat-trick goals came in the 7th, 32nd and 57th minutes, each one a different flavour of striker's excellence: a cool finish, a header, and a clinical slot past the keeper from a tight angle.

By the time he completed his hat-trick, Lens had long since capitulated. The floodgates opened with the first goal and simply never closed.

The full Como scorecard:
- **Douvikas** (7', 32', 57') — hat-trick
- **Paz** (40')
- **Da Cunha** (47')
- **Kempf** (53')
- **Liberali** (62')
- **Baturina** (69')
- **Papaccioli** (90+1')

Lens' only response came via **Ruben Aguilar** in the 67th minute — a moment that served mainly to remind everyone watching that this was actually a football match and not a training drill.

The final scoreline of 9–1 (from Lens' perspective: 1–9) represents:
- The **joint-biggest winning margin** (8 goals) in Champions League history
- The **first nine-goal away performance** in the competition's history
- The **joint-highest scoring CL match** result of the modern era

---

## Who Is This Como Side?

If you haven't been following Serie A this season, Como might feel like a name that appeared from nowhere. They didn't. This has been one of the great footballing rise stories of the past five years.

Como 1907 — to use their full name — were promoted to Serie A for the first time in 21 years at the end of the 2023-24 season. Rather than consolidating, they finished in the top six in their debut campaign, earning a historic first European berth. But even their most fervent supporters couldn't have imagined this.

The club has been backed by Sent Entertainment (the Indonesian group who purchased them in 2019) and has built intelligently — mixing seasoned European veterans with exciting young talent. Their squad blends Italian creativity with proven international quality, and Douvikas has been the focal point all season.

Their Champions League journey this season has been one of the most joyful stories in European football. And now they've made history.

---

## The Records Broken

For context on just how significant this is:

- **Biggest CL away wins ever (8-goal margin):** This now sits alongside a tiny handful of matches from the group stage era.
- **Nine goals in an away game** had never happened in the Champions League before Wednesday night.
- In 32 years of the Champions League, no visiting side had managed this.

It's the kind of record that feels like it should be unbreakable. But then again, before Wednesday, so did the previous one.

---

## What Comes Next for Como?

Realistically, Como are still unlikely to progress from their group. Three defeats before this result leave them with a mountain to climb in the remaining matches. But that is almost beside the point now.

This performance will attract global attention, transfer interest, and — most importantly for a club still building its identity on the European stage — an enormous amount of respect.

Douvikas will be on every major club's radar by the time the January window opens. The 26-year-old Greek international, who joined from Utrecht in 2024, has been one of the best strikers in Serie A and has now announced himself emphatically to a continental audience.

---

## A Final Thought

Football has a habit of throwing up moments that feel impossible until they happen and inevitable once they have. Como 9-1 Lens is one of those moments.

A club promoted from Serie B three years ago. Playing in the Champions League. Scoring nine goals away from home. Breaking records set over 32 years of elite European competition.

If you needed a reminder of why this sport is extraordinary, Wednesday night in Lens gave you nine of them.
