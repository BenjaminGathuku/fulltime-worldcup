Premier League Matchday 10 delivered two of the most significant stories of the 2026-27 season so far: Arsenal extended their lead at the top while Manchester United's managerial crisis accelerated dramatically following a chaotic 4-4 draw with Aston Villa that leaves them 18th in the table.

## Arsenal 1-0 Hull City

Routine work from the league leaders. Arsenal got the job done against Hull City with a 1-0 win that keeps them five points clear at the top. The performance was not spectacular, but it was controlled. A team that leads by five points after ten games does not need to be spectacular against Hull. It needs three points and minimal injury risk, and that is what it got.

Arsenal sit on 27 points from 10 games, a goal difference of plus ten. The gap to second place has become significant enough that even a run of dropped points would not immediately threaten their position.

## Manchester United 4-4 Aston Villa: The Result That Changes Everything

This is not a result, it is an event. Manchester United drew 4-4 with Aston Villa at Old Trafford, conceding four goals at home in a league match that leaves them with more goals conceded than any team in Europe's top five leagues this season. The draw keeps them in 18th place, one point above the bottom three.

Carrick's side reportedly came back into the game after going behind, suggesting there is spirit in the squad. But a team that cannot defend well enough to win at home against a mid-table side is a team in structural trouble, not situational trouble.

Reports published on 8 and 9 November indicate that United have booked a decision meeting about Carrick's future during the international break. The two-week pause will be used to make a final assessment. The article from Centre Devils, citing multiple sources, stated United will decide before the Christmas fixture run. Eddie Howe is reportedly the leading candidate to replace him.

## Nottingham Forest 3-1 Manchester City

The result that rattled City. Nottingham Forest beat them 3-1 at the City Ground, a performance that raised serious questions about City's domestic consistency this season. They have dropped points against Brighton and now Forest in recent weeks, and the gap between their Champions League form and their league form is becoming increasingly hard to explain.

Forest climb to 16th on 9 points. City slip to third on 21 points, still well placed in the title race but no longer the automatic second contender.

## Crystal Palace 1-3 Liverpool

Liverpool got back to winning ways after their defeat to Arsenal. A 3-1 win at Crystal Palace lifts them to fourth on 18 points, back into the conversation for the top four. Palace remain bottom of the table on 7 points and are showing little sign of the improvement needed to escape relegation trouble.

## The Rest of Matchday 10

Leeds United beat Tottenham 1-0, a result that pushed Spurs to 11th on 12 points and kept Leeds in the top half on 14 points. Sunderland beat Chelsea 1-0, a surprise result that showed Chelsea's away inconsistency. Brighton beat Brentford 2-0 and sit second on 22 points, their best-ever league position after ten games.

Everton beat Coventry 3-1 on the Friday night. Fulham and Newcastle drew 1-1. Bournemouth beat Ipswich 2-1.

## The Table After Matchday 10

Arsenal lead on 27 points. Brighton have emerged as the unexpected second-placed side on 22 points, with Manchester City third on 21. Liverpool are fourth. Chelsea fifth. At the bottom, Crystal Palace, Sunderland and Manchester United are separated by one point in the relegation positions.

The international break arrives at a critical moment for United. By the time the Premier League resumes with United's trip to Liverpool, the club will have made a decision on who is in charge. The only question is what that decision will be.
