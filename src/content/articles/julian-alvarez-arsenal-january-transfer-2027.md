Julian Alvarez has reportedly given Arsenal the green light for a January 2027 transfer, ending months of uncertainty around a move that the Gunners have been pursuing since before the summer window. The Argentine's change of heart follows the collapse of his preferred move to Barcelona, leaving Arsenal as his clearest route to one of Europe's elite clubs.

## How We Got Here

The story of Alvarez and Arsenal has been unfolding since early 2026. Arteta identified the Atletico Madrid striker as his primary attacking target, a player whose combination of goals, pressing and international pedigree made him the right fit for Arsenal's system.

Atletico were prepared to sell at around 150 million euros. Arsenal were prepared to pay it. The obstacle was Alvarez himself, who was holding out for Barcelona. He had spoken publicly about his admiration for the club and was willing to stay at Atletico rather than move somewhere that was not his first choice.

That stance held through the summer. Arsenal pursued other options, the summer window closed without a deal, and Alvarez remained in Madrid.

## What Changed

Barcelona's pursuit of Erling Haaland changed the equation. The Spanish club moved for the Norwegian striker as their priority attacking signing, effectively removing themselves from the Alvarez conversation. With Barcelona no longer a realistic option, Alvarez reassessed.

TEAMtalk reported on 7 October that Alvarez was now open to Arsenal. A further update on 26 October, one day before the Ballon d'Or ceremony, stated that Atletico Madrid had given their verdict on his January future. Guillem Balague separately reported that Alvarez now genuinely wants the move and is seriously considering making it happen in January.

The shift from "open to it" to "wants it" is significant. A player who wanted Barcelona and was using Arsenal as a fallback is a different negotiating partner from a player who has accepted that this is his move and is committed to making it work.

## What Arsenal Are Offering

The reports indicate Arsenal are willing to include players in a deal to reduce the cash element. Noni Madueke and Viktor Gyokeres have been mentioned as potential makeweights, though the specific package has not been confirmed. A straight cash deal in the region of 150 million euros remains possible.

Arteta has reportedly been directly involved in personal conversations with Alvarez, taking the negotiations beyond the club's sporting director and into direct player-to-manager communication. That level of personal engagement from the manager reflects how highly Arsenal rate this target and how determined they are not to lose him again.

## What Alvarez Gives Arsenal

Arsenal have been strong this season, top of the Premier League after nine games and perfect in the Champions League. But their attacking profile lacks a pure centre-forward of the highest quality. The goals have been spread across the squad rather than concentrated in one dominant striker.

Alvarez is a different type of forward from the classic number nine, but his work rate, technical quality and ability to score consistently across all competitions makes him transformative for any team he joins. At Manchester City, before his move to Atletico, he won the Champions League and the World Cup in the same year.

He is 26 years old. He is at the peak of his powers. Arsenal have been trying to sign him for most of the year. If January delivers what October's reports suggest it might, Arteta will have his primary target in the building before the second half of the season begins.

## The Remaining Questions

Nothing is confirmed. Personal terms have not been announced. The specific fee structure has not been reported. Atletico Madrid's public position is that they will assess his situation, which is not the same as agreeing to sell in January.

The direction of travel is toward Arsenal. Whether the direction and the destination are the same thing will become clearer over the next eight weeks.
