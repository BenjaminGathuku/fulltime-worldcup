With Champions League football providing fresh scouting opportunities and the January window approaching, Premier League clubs are already making moves in the market. Here's a full rundown of the most credible transfer stories doing the rounds this week.

---

## Arsenal Eye Antonio Nusa — And Have the Perfect Lure

**Antonio Nusa** (21, RB Leipzig / Norway) is emerging as one of the most coveted wingers in European football, and Arsenal are positioning themselves as the most likely destination for a January or summer move.

The Norwegian international — who starred at the 2026 World Cup — joined Leipzig for €21m and has been outstanding in the Bundesliga. His pace, directness and ability to play on either flank make him the prototype modern wide attacker, and multiple clubs have been monitoring him.

Bayern Munich's interest has cooled significantly, according to transfer expert Christian Falk. The stumbling block? Nusa wants to start. Bayern were seeking a backup to Luis Díaz, and the valuation being discussed — in the €50-60m bracket — was considered too steep for a rotation player. Nusa's camp made it clear: he is "no backup."

That opens the door for Arsenal, whose interest looks increasingly realistic. The Gunners have a compelling pitch: they're Premier League champions, unbeaten in the Champions League this season, and — crucially — have **Martin Odegaard** already at the club. The Norwegian captain is considered one of Nusa's biggest admirers and would almost certainly be part of any recruitment conversation.

A source close to the deal told CaughtOffside this week that Arsenal's chances of landing Nusa are "more realistic than Bayern's", with spring the most likely window for a decision.

**Verdict:** Watch this space. If Arsenal can get this deal done in January, it would be a statement signing.

---

## Arsenal Also Monitoring Tavernier (£26m)

A second Arsenal target has emerged in the form of **Marcus Tavernier** (27, Bournemouth). The versatile attacking midfielder — capable of playing in multiple roles — has been in excellent form, with six goals in 14 appearances so far this season.

Bournemouth want to keep him and would demand around £26m. Arsenal, Manchester United, Newcastle and Tottenham are all said to be watching. With Arteta looking to add creative depth, Tavernier offers flexibility and proven Premier League quality.

The competition for his signature means Arsenal may need to move quickly or risk a bidding war.

---

## Newcastle Want Ivan Toney Back from Saudi Arabia

Here's a story with a compelling World Cup connection. **Ivan Toney** (30) left for Al-Ahli in the Saudi Pro League after a controversial exit from English football — but his time in the desert may be coming to an end faster than anticipated.

Newcastle United, who are struggling for goals, have identified the England striker as their top January target. Toney was one of England's key performers at the 2026 World Cup this summer, and there's a growing sense in the game that his best football belongs back in Europe.

Newcastle manager Matthias Jaissle is believed to be a fan, and the relationship there could be key to any deal. Manchester United are also monitoring the situation.

For Al-Ahli, the financial calculus may favour a sale — Saudi clubs have often shown willingness to let players return when the right offer arrives. A fee in the region of £30-40m is being discussed.

**Verdict:** This one could happen. Toney will be motivated to prove himself before the 2027 World Cup qualifying cycle begins in earnest.

---

## Liverpool Eye Archie Brown — The Man Who Beat Them

This one has a nice piece of irony to it. **Archie Brown** — the 24-year-old Fenerbahçe left-back who scored the goal that levelled things at 2-2 before Muriqi's winner sealed Liverpool's first Champions League defeat on Wednesday — is now reportedly a Liverpool transfer target.

The Englishman has been outstanding this season, combining assured defending with a willingness to get forward and contribute in the final third. Liverpool are looking for competition and long-term cover for their left-back position, and Brown fits the profile.

The complication? He's not alone on the radar. Manchester United, Chelsea and Newcastle are all said to be watching the former youth product, and Fenerbahçe will not let him go cheaply after the season he's having.

A fee of around £35-40m is being discussed for a player whose Champions League performance this week gave him the biggest shop window of his career.

**Verdict:** Liverpool might actually turn this one into a signing. The connection is there, the need is real, and beating them 3-2 in a CL group game is exactly the kind of statement that accelerates a move.

---

## The Bigger Picture: January Will Be Busy

What's notable about all of these stories is the common thread: Premier League clubs with European ambitions are shopping for quality, not just squad depth.

Arsenal, with two competitions still to win, want creative wide players. Newcastle want a striker who can deliver in big games. Liverpool want defensive cover that fits their system.

The 2026 World Cup acted as a global showcase — Toney, Nusa, Brown and others all raised their profiles significantly this summer. The January window will give clubs a chance to act on what they saw. Expect it to be one of the busiest in recent memory.
