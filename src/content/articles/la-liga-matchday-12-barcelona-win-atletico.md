Barcelona moved three points clear at the top of La Liga with one of the more significant results of the season so far. A 2-1 win at Atletico Madrid, sealed by a Fermin Lopez brace, tells the story of a team that can win the big matches away from home when it matters. Real Madrid beat Valencia 3-0 to stay in touch, but the gap has widened.

## Atletico Madrid 1-2 Barcelona: The Result That Defines the Week

Atletico Madrid at the Metropolitano is never easy. Under Diego Simeone the ground is as difficult a place to get a result as anywhere in Europe, and Barcelona arrived there needing to win to stay clear of both Atletico and Real Madrid.

Fermin Lopez was the difference. Two goals from the midfielder gave Barcelona a 2-1 victory after Atletico had pulled one back to make it tense. The comeback never arrived. Barcelona held, left Madrid with three points, and sit on 33 points from 12 games.

The result is a statement because of where it came from. Barcelona winning at Atletico's ground in a match where both teams needed the points confirms that this is the best side in Spain right now.

## Valencia 0-3 Real Madrid

Real Madrid went to Valencia and produced a clinical 3-0 win, the kind of authoritative away performance that keeps them in the title conversation. They are second on 30 points, three behind Barcelona, and they have shown across this matchday that they are not about to drop out of contention.

The gap at the top is three points with 26 games left. Real Madrid will expect to close it. They will need to, because Barcelona have shown no sign of inconsistency.

## The Rest of Matchday 12

Real Betis beat Elche 2-0 to climb to fourth on 26 points, their best position in the table for some time. Real Sociedad beat Rayo Vallecano 1-0, Villarreal beat Getafe 2-0. Osasuna and Athletic Club drew 1-1. Espanyol and Deportivo La Coruña drew 0-0. Sevilla and Alavés also drew goalless on the Friday night, a result that keeps both clubs in the mid-table.

Malaga's difficulties continue. A 2-1 defeat to Racing Santander leaves them bottom of the table on 5 points with a goal difference of minus seventeen. After twelve games, the gap to safety is growing.

## The Table After Matchday 12

Barcelona lead on 33 points. Real Madrid second on 30. Atletico Madrid third on 28, having now lost ground to both of the teams above them. Real Betis fourth on 26 is the story at the other end of the top of the table, with the Seville club making a genuine push for European places.

At the bottom, Malaga, Elche and Valencia are the three clubs in the relegation positions. Valencia's 3-0 defeat at home keeps them in the bottom three, and the club's domestic season is becoming a serious cause for concern.
