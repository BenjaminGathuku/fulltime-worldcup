Matchday 4 of the 2026-27 Champions League has produced its clearest picture yet of who the serious contenders are and who is running out of time to save their campaign. Bayern Munich, Arsenal and Inter Milan stand alone at the top on 12 points. The chasing pack looks increasingly crowded.

## The Headline Result: Bayern Beat PSG

Bayern Munich went to Paris and won 2-1, a result that tells you something significant about the gap between who PSG want to be and where they currently are in Europe. Luis Diaz scored twice for the visitors before being sent off, and even with ten men Bayern held on after Joao Neves pulled one back for PSG.

Diaz scoring two and then getting sent off is the kind of performance that generates match reports for days. The German side, who beat PSG with a man advantage for most of the second half, top the Champions League table outright on 12 points. PSG are fifth, still in the qualifying positions but with questions forming around their defensive structure and their ability to compete with Europe's best when the knockout rounds arrive.

Dembele and Hakimi both picked up injuries during the match, adding a squad management concern to the tactical one.

## Liverpool 1-0 Real Madrid

Alexis Mac Allister scored at the hour mark and Thibaut Courtois made eight saves. That is the story of Liverpool's win over Real Madrid at Anfield in Matchday 4. Eight saves from the goalkeeper who prevented this from becoming an embarrassment. One goal from the midfielder who decided the game.

Liverpool are building something in this competition. They sit above Real Madrid in the table after four games, which is not a position anyone expected at the start of the phase. Real Madrid, without a consistent defensive structure and with Valverde picking up an injury in this game, face Matchday 5 needing a response.

## Arsenal's Perfect Record Continues

Arsenal beat Slavia Praha 3-0 and remain level with Bayern and Inter on 12 points. Bukayo Saka scored an early penalty. Mikel Merino added a brace in the second half. It was a professional performance against modest opposition, the kind Arsenal need to deliver without expending the energy required for the bigger games ahead.

Merino finished the match rated 8.7 by SofaScore, the best outfield performer of the entire round. His second-half double reflected a player who has settled into Arteta's system and is producing consistently across competitions.

## Barcelona's Dropped Points

Barcelona drew 3-3 with Club Brugge at the Camp Nou, dropping two points that have them sitting 11th in the Champions League table. Tresoldi and Ferran Torres gave them a two-goal lead. Carlos Forbs scored twice for the visitors to level it. Lamine Yamal put Barcelona back ahead, only for Christos Tzolis to put through an own goal in the closing stages that levelled it again.

A 3-3 at home to Brugge is a result that creates genuine concern. Barcelona are not in the bottom section yet, but they are not in a comfortable position either. Lamine Yamal was among their best performers, rated 8.4, which at least suggests the platform for a turnaround exists.

## Manchester City 4-1 Dortmund

Phil Foden earned a perfect 10 from SofaScore. He scored twice, provided the energy and creativity that City's performance was built on, and produced the best individual display of the entire round. Rayan Cherki also scored as City ran out comfortable winners.

City sit fourth in the table, behind Bayern, Arsenal, Inter and above PSG. Foden's form is the most encouraging sign at a club that has been managing the transition from one era to the next.
