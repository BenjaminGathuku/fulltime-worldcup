Serie A Matchday 11 maintained the competitive picture at the top of Italian football with a mixture of decisive wins and dropped points from the leading sides. Juventus, who beat Napoli last weekend to go top, could not hold on for three points against Fiorentina. Inter kept pace with a controlled win over Como. AC Milan continued their strong form away at Genoa.

## Fiorentina 2-2 Juventus: Points Dropped

The match of the round at the top of the table. Juventus came to Florence needing a win to cement their position and left with a draw that allowed the teams below them to close the gap. A 2-2 result represents dropped points for the Turin side given what was at stake, though Fiorentina will rightly take credit for forcing the comeback.

The draw means Juventus cannot afford many more slip-ups if they want to stay in first place as the season approaches the November international break.

## Inter 2-0 Como

Inter Milan did the professional job against newly promoted Como, winning 2-0 at the San Siro. The result keeps Inter in contention at the top of the table and maintains the kind of consistency that has kept them as a title threat across the first eleven weeks.

Como, who are building a reputation as one of the more attractive newly promoted sides in recent Serie A memory, could not translate their possession into the goals needed.

## Genoa 1-2 AC Milan

AC Milan continued their impressive run of form with a 2-1 win at Genoa. Following last weekend's derby victory over Inter, three more points on the road confirms that this is a Milan side with genuine title ambitions rather than just a one-off performance. The win at Genoa required resilience after the home side pulled one back, but Milan managed the game to its conclusion.

## Napoli 1-1 Lazio

A score draw between two of the clubs expected to challenge near the top. Napoli needed a win after last week's defeat to Juventus, but Lazio held them to 1-1 in a match that keeps the gap between the top of the table and the chasing pack smaller than it might otherwise have been.

## Roma 3-0 Sassuolo

Roma put three past Sassuolo without reply, a performance that keeps them in touch with the top of the table and confirms the form they have been building. Donyell Malen is among their top scorers this season on 8 goals, one of the reasons their attacking record has been among the best in the division.

## The Rest of Matchday 11

Venezia beat Udinese 2-1 on the Friday night. Cagliari beat Frosinone 1-0. Torino and Lecce drew 1-1. Parma lost 1-2 to Bologna. Monza lost 0-1 to Atalanta, leaving Monza's top scorer Gustavo Varela, who leads the entire league with 10 goals, unable to influence an away defeat for his side.

## The Top Scorer Picture

Gustavo Varela of Monza leads the Golden Boot race on 10 goals from 11 games, a remarkable return for a player at a mid-table club. Roma's Donyell Malen and Frosinone's Antonio Raimondo are joint second on 8 goals each.
